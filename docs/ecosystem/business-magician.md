# Business-Magician

**Business-Magician** is the AI-powered business platform for the MBTQ ecosystem, built on 360 Magicians AI.

It was originally part of MBTQ.dev and has been spun out as a focused service.

## What it provides

- ✓ Idea Validation & Market Research
- ✓ Business Plan Generation
- ✓ Growth Strategy Planning
- ✓ Managed Services for Entrepreneurs
- ✓ Deaf Entrepreneur Support

## Relationship to MBTQ.dev

Business-Magician is an ecosystem service — MBTQ.dev provides the developer platform, tooling, and accessibility infrastructure that Business-Magician builds on.

Think of it as: MBTQ.dev is the **developer hub**; Business-Magician is a **focused application** in the ecosystem.
